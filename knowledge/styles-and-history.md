# Styles and history

- Copy styles is Ctrl+Alt+C (Cmd+Option+C on macOS) and paste styles is Ctrl+Alt+V (Cmd+Option+V). (source: excalidraw source at commit 4c00f31 (2026-10-08): actions/actionStyles.ts lines 78-79 and 229-230; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- Undo is Ctrl+Z; redo is Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y (Cmd on macOS). (source: excalidraw source at commit 4c00f31 (2026-10-08): actions/actionHistory.tsx lines 79-80 and 118-120; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- Ctrl+D duplicates the selection, and Alt+drag (Option+drag) drags out a copy. (source: excalidraw source at commit 4c00f31 (2026-10-08): actions/actionDuplicateSelection.tsx line 119; components/App.tsx line 9631 (Alt while dragging); accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
