# Pending knowledge: issue 99001

Not in the product yet. Becomes current behavior when the issue ships.

- [text-properties] Underline is one property of the whole text element, turned on and off by an "Underline" toggle next to the text align control (tooltip "Underline (Ctrl+U)"), shown when a text or a shape with text is selected or the Text tool is active; there is no partial underline. (source: Approved answers 3 and 11 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] Ctrl+U (Cmd+U on macOS) toggles underline on the selected text or on the text being edited, even when only part of it is highlighted; with nothing selected it does nothing, and the browser's view-source never opens while the canvas has focus. (source: Approved answers 1 to 3 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] With several texts selected and only some underlined, the "Underline" toggle shows as not pressed; one click underlines all of them and a second click removes the underline from all. (source: Approved answer 5 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] Every line of an underlined text is underlined under that line's own width, following its alignment, in the text's color and opacity, with a thickness that scales with font size, in every script including right-to-left text and emoji, never clipped. (source: Approved answers 7 and 8 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] The last chosen underline value becomes the default for new text. (source: Approved answer 6 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
- [bound-text] Selecting a shape or sticky note that holds text and using the "Underline" toggle or Ctrl+U underlines its text; a sticky note's auto-fit size and line breaks are unaffected by underline. (source: Approved answer 4 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
- [styles-and-history] Paste styles, Ctrl+C then Ctrl+V, Ctrl+D, Alt+drag and library items carry a text's underline, and undo/redo restores it. (source: Approved answer 10 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
- [files-and-export] Underline survives a page reload, a saved .excalidraw file, PNG and SVG export, copy as PNG/SVG and live collaboration; an older Excalidraw version opens a file with underlined text without error and shows the text without underline. (source: Approved answer 9 in the issue 99001 session, 2026-10-11; accepted 2026-10-11 by maxxx)
