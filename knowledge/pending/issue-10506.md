# Pending knowledge: issue 10506

Not in the product yet. Becomes current behavior when the issue ships.

- [text-properties] Two font size presets are added after "Very large": "Huge" = 56 and "Giant" = 96; the existing four presets keep their labels and sizes, and there is no typed font size field. (source: Answer on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] When the selected text is at a size between presets, or several selected texts have different sizes, no font size preset is highlighted until one is clicked, and clicking a preset sets that exact size on all of them. (source: Answer on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] The increase and decrease font size shortcuts do not change with the "Huge" and "Giant" presets and have no upper limit at "Giant". (source: Answer on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] A text at exactly 56 or 96, however it got there (corner resize, shortcut, typed size, pasted styles or an opened file), shows "Huge" or "Giant" highlighted. (source: Approved answer 2 on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] The "Huge" and "Giant" presets have tooltips "Huge" and "Giant", like the existing presets, and no keyboard shortcuts of their own. (source: Approved answer 3 on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- [bound-text] "Huge" and "Giant" are offered for sticky note labels; auto-fit is unchanged, and the picked preset stays highlighted even when the label is shown smaller to fit. (source: Approved answer 5 on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] All six font size presets fit a 375 px wide properties panel in the mobile layout without horizontal scrolling or overlap. (source: Approved answer 6 on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
- [text-properties] In locales other than English, the "Huge" and "Giant" labels fall back to English, never to an empty label or a raw key name. (source: Approved answer 7 on issue 10506, 2026-10-10, approved by maxxx; accepted 2026-10-10 by maxxx)
